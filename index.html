Process

What commissioning looks like

Five steps from first message to final file, usually 1-2 days depending heavily on scope.

1

Reaching out & Inquiry

You send a DETAILED description, deadline, budget range, and a reference. I reply within 24 hours with details.

2

Quote & deposit

I send a fixed quote and estimated delivery. A 50% refundable deposit secures your spot on the schedule. A 50% upfront deposit may not be refunded if the project has already begun.

3

Sketch

Within 48 hours of the beginning of the project I will send a rough, untextured image of your model. This is the time to give feedback and request a change.

4

Render & revise

Upon completion, you may be sent a simple render of the finished product showcasing the model. You will then be given 3 free revisions including color changes and minor adjustments. An additional revision will result in a 5$ fee. At this point in the commission you MAY NOT request a revamp of the model.

5

Delivery

Once I've sent a render with your completed revision you will be given 24 hours to respond. If I do not receive a response I will assume you are not interested anymore and pause or cancel the commission. Once you respond you will be required to pay the remaining 50% balance. Once completed I will deliver a high resolution, game optimized, Fbx, Obj or any other file formate to you.
